# MLKit baseline – findings (Section 2)

**Setup:** MLKit Text Recognition v2, bundled Latin model (`com.google.mlkit:text-recognition:16.0.1`,
`DEFAULT_OPTIONS`), called through the app's own `ppg_ocr` channel – no changes to settings.
Device: Realme RMX3710, Android 14, MediaTek Helio G85 (MT6769H). 50 images, 3 runs each.
Run date: 25 Oct 2026. Scoring is strict (exact characters) at OCR level, before any parsing.

Cold start (first call, model load): **3.8 s**.

## What goes wrong (216 field misses)

1. **`ID` is read as `1D` in material numbers** – 49 of 74 material misses (another 7 drop the `I`).
   `517705ID2176` → `5177051D2176`. Systematic, so a post-processing rule fixes it
   (`\d{6}1D\d{4}` → `ID`). Happens even on clean photos.
2. **Low light produces *wrong but valid-looking* articles** – `475211` → `476211`, `446789` → `448789`,
   `445365` → `445385`. These are the most dangerous errors: they can match a different real
   article in the master list. Low light is the only condition that hurt article recall.
3. **The `RED / SIGMA 6 8 PC` trap fires** – MLKit returns `68 PC` (3 of 5 images). Any parser that
   reads the number before `PC` will record 68 instead of 8.
4. **DN number cut off at the box border** – `5319364114` → `53193641` on 5 DN2 images, where the
   last digits touch the printed box line.
5. **Short isolated table cells are skipped** – e.g. `60 L` and `80 L` on DN1 p3 are not detected
   at all; MLKit drops lone short tokens in sparse columns.
6. **`/` in `Page : 2 / 6` read as `I` or `|`** – 12 of 19 page-marker misses.

What MLKit handled well: shadows, creases and moderate glare barely changed results (≈88–91% field
recall, 100% article recall). The faint `85.568 KG` was read correctly.

## Latency breakdown (median per image)

| Step | Time |
|---|---|
| Decode image (1200×1600) | ~20 ms |
| MLKit recognition | ~480 ms |
| Building result + channel transfer to Dart | ~250 ms |
| **Round trip** | **~750 ms** |

- About a third of the round trip happens *after* MLKit finishes: `PpgOcrPlugin` builds the
  symbol list through Java reflection for every word and sends lines, words and symbols across the
  channel. This is a cheap target for optimisation (call `element.symbols` directly, or skip symbols).
- Per-page OCR time is not the bottleneck for the 1–2 minute target: a 6-page note costs ~4.5 s of
  OCR plus a one-time 3.8 s cold start (warm-up at app start removes it).

## Caveats

- 40 of 50 images are simulated conditions from 10 real photos (see `../../README.md`).
- Photos are 1200×1600 (compressed in transfer). Full-resolution 12 MP camera output will be slower
  to decode and recognise – re-measure latency with phone-original images.
- Recall is measured on raw OCR text; the app's parser and master-list lookup may recover or add errors.
